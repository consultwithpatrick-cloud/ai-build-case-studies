# AI Build Case Studies

Selected case studies of AI systems I designed and built end to end. Each write-up focuses on the problem, the approach, and the outcome. Client details are anonymized, and no code, prompts, or proprietary specifics are included.

Built by **Patrick Joseph Panuncillon**, Director for AI Strategy and Growth.

## Case studies

- **[AI SMS Sales Assistant](ai-sms-sales-assistant.html)** — A human-in-the-loop reply assistant for a home-services company's SMS sales team. Drafts are grounded in the team's own conversation history and retrieved on the fly; a routing and caching layer keeps running cost low; a nightly learning loop keeps the voice current. A human reviews and sends every reply.

- **[AI Reply-Training Console](ai-reply-training-console.html)** — A safe training environment that captures a distinct voice for each department without touching production. It ingests conversations, anchors per-department voice, and drafts reply options with full-conversation context.
