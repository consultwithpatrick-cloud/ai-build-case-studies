## 4. Confidentiality

Every page in this repository is written to one fixed disclosure standard. The following are withheld without exception:

1. The name of the employer or client, and any detail sufficient to identify them
2. Source code, in whole or in part
3. Prompts, prompt templates, and system instructions
4. Credentials, keys, tokens, endpoints, hostnames, and internal identifiers
5. Schemas, data models, and internal record structures
6. Customer data of any kind, in any form, including anonymized or composited
7. Named third-party vendors in the client's operational stack
8. Exact commercial figures, contract terms, and pricing
9. Screenshots or recordings of live systems
10. Any material subject to a confidentiality obligation

Figures that appear are rounded, banded, or expressed as ranges, and appear only where the band itself carries no confidential information. Where an honest description of a result would require a confidential figure, the result is omitted rather than approximated into something misleading.

Third-party vendors in a client's operational stack are described by category, for example "a managed vector database" or "a field-service platform", rather than by name. A company's tooling choices are its own information to disclose.

All diagrams are drawn in CSS from public description. No screenshot, export, log, or artefact from any live system appears in this repository.
